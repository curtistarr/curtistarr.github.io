Welcome to my new blog. I've spent the last while collecting thoughts in scratch
files and never quite shipping them, so I'm giving them a permanent home here.

## What to expect

I'll be writing about a few topics that keep showing up in my work and
side projects:

- **Tools and tooling.** The small, sharp utilities that make the day-to-day
  better, and the bigger systems that quietly hold everything up.
- **Engineering practice.** What "good code" actually means in the contexts
  I work in, and the trade-offs I keep running into.
- **Side projects.** Half-finished games, infrastructure experiments, and
  whatever else I've been tinkering with on weekends.

Posts will be short by default. If a thought needs more room I'll let it
have more room, but I'd rather publish a useful 300 words than sit on a
2,000-word draft forever.

Thanks for stopping by. More soon.
